The Problem: The 48-Hour Space Spectroscopy Bottleneck

1. The Cluster Compute Crisis

Observing atmospheric water and methane with transmission spectroscopy requires heavy MCMC Bayesian sampling (TauREx 3) taking 12 to 48 hours of CPU cluster time per planet.

2. Opto-Mechanical Pointing Drift

Telescope pointing jitter in space introduces chromatic noise that dilutes transit depths by up to 200 ppm.

3. The Unocculted Spot Dilemma

Cool stellar spots (4200 K) mimic the 2.7 µm water vapor band, causing false alarms in black-box neural networks.

The Hardware Architecture: 8-Parameter Orthogonal Inversion

We couple sensor telemetry (AIRS 52-channel spectroscopy + FGS pointing jitter) into an 8-parameter joint design matrix: M = [1, x, y, x², y², xy, -Transit(t), Spot(t)].

By solving Generalized Least Squares on GPU Tensor Cores via batched GEMM operations, transit depth is solved simultaneously with pointing drift, mathematically eliminating transit dilution.
